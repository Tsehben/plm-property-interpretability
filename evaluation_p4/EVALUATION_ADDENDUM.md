# P4 evaluation — addendum: levels behind the paired differences

Closes out the frozen P4 intervention experiment. **Nothing in `v1/` has been
changed.** This addendum is derived from the saved per-protein outputs only; no
model was run, no protein was added, no setting was altered. Generated by
[paired_levels_addendum.py](paired_levels_addendum.py); outputs in
[v1_addendum/](v1_addendum/).

## 1. Candidate and matched-control levels on the exact paired observations

Each row uses the **same cells and the same aggregation** as the reported
difference: available matched controls averaged within a (protein, position)
cell → candidate, control and difference averaged within protein over that
protein's paired positions → averaged across proteins. Because the mean is
linear over an identical cell set, `mean candidate − mean control` reproduces
the reported estimate exactly; this is asserted in code, and all four rows
reconstruct to the last representable digit.

| candidate | edit | n prot | n paired pos | mean candidate KL (nats) | mean control KL (nats) | mean paired difference | cand/ctrl |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2564** | **×0** | **61** | **61** | **4.799e-05** | **2.702e-05** | **+2.097e-05** | **1.78** |
| 2564 | ×2 | 136 | 202 | 2.859e-04 | 2.029e-04 | +8.301e-05 | 1.41 |
| 3900 | ×0 | 275 | 461 | 6.960e-05 | 6.319e-05 | +6.413e-06 | 1.10 |
| 3900 | ×2 | 301 | 706 | 2.229e-04 | 1.524e-04 | +7.050e-05 | 1.46 |

Medians over the same proteins, which the means do not describe:

| candidate | edit | median candidate KL | median control KL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2564 | ×0 | 2.201e-05 | 1.453e-05 |
| 2564 | ×2 | 9.461e-05 | 5.874e-05 |
| 3900 | ×0 | 1.601e-05 | **2.010e-05** |
| 3900 | ×2 | 3.589e-05 | **4.439e-05** |

For both of 3900's contrasts the **typical** protein's matched controls move the
distribution *more* than the candidate does, while the means run the other way.
That is the same right-tail asymmetry the main report flagged, now visible in
the levels rather than only in the difference. For 2564 the candidate is above
its controls at both the mean and the median.

**These candidate means are not comparable with the all-sampled candidate means
in [v1/all_sampled_candidate_outcomes.tsv](v1/all_sampled_candidate_outcomes.tsv).**
The all-sampled series covers every active candidate cell, including the
majority that has no feasible matched control; the series above covers only
paired cells. The two populations differ, and the difference between them is a
selection effect of control feasibility, not an effect of the edit. No such
comparison is made here and none should be made downstream.

## 2. The 2564-ablation result is conditional on feasible control matching

> **Conditional on feasible control matching.** Ablating feature 2564 at a
> masked N-terminal residue changes the model's masked-token distribution more
> than norm-matched control features do, by +2.10e-05 nats (95% BCa
> [+1.13e-05, +3.50e-05]; Bonferroni-adjusted 98.75% [+9.05e-06, +4.01e-05]),
> **on the subset of observations for which a norm-matched control could be
> constructed at all.**

Coverage of that subset:

| | |
|---|---|
| candidate-active cells | 332 of 1,200 |
| **paired cells** | **61 (18.4% of active cells — the narrowest coverage in the design)** |
| proteins contributing | 61 of 400 |
| class split | **55 Extracellular, 6 Cytoplasm** |
| position split | **10 → 3 cells; 20 → 50 cells; 30 → 8 cells** |
| Extracellular share at position 20 | 49 of 50 |
| mean controls per paired cell | 1.15 |

The loss of coverage is structural, not random. Matching an ablation's realised
residual-space norm requires a large *negative* latent change in the control
feature, which frequently exceeds the control's own latent value; those
conditions are marked unavailable. This removes preferentially the cells where
the candidate's latent was large relative to the control's. So the paired
population is not a random subsample of the active population, and the estimate
describes the feasible-control subset — which is predominantly Extracellular
proteins edited at residue 20.

The six Cytoplasm proteins are too few to support any statement; their
class-specific interval contains zero.

## 3. What this result is not used for

The next study's candidate is **not** selected from this result. Candidate 1446
remains the primary proposal for the sequence-variant work because it was chosen
during discovery, on P1+P2, as a representative signal-associated feature,
before any P4 output existed. Promoting 2564 because its P4 contrast excluded
zero would select a candidate on the outcome of the same evaluation that is
meant to be independent of it, and would also inherit the coverage restriction
above. See [../sequence_variant_pilot/PILOT_SPEC.md](../sequence_variant_pilot/PILOT_SPEC.md).
